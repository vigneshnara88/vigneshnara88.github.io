Missing data in Kuwait's air-quality network

Understanding station coverage, sustained outages and the modelling challenges of PM2.5 concentrations.

Area
Data science
Methods & tools
Python · Environmental data

The data problem

Missing environmental measurements are not always isolated gaps. Stations may have prolonged outages, incompatible naming conventions or no sensor for a particular pollutant. These distinctions affect both imputation and the interpretation of a prediction model.

Technical work

The project materials include missingness visualisation, station harmonisation, time-series exploration, regression diagnostics and studies of imputation under sustained outages. Modelling choices must account for spikes and the temporal and spatial structure of the observations.

Collaboration

The research proposal identifies Dr Ghita Berrada as principal investigator and Dr Huda Alrashidi as co-principal investigator. Additional collaborators' contributions, including Oskar's modelling work, will be credited in any detailed release. Research code and data are subject to permission before publication.
