Independent exploration

Family background in longitudinal data

Constructing multiwave household data to explore adoption, family background and socioeconomic outcomes.

Area
Independent projects
Methods & tools
Stata · UKHLS
View code on GitHub ↗

Data construction

The Stata workflow links person and household records across UKHLS waves, harmonises adoption and stepchild fields, and prepares socioeconomic measures. The project is motivated by the difficulties of separating family circumstances, selection and individual outcomes.

Interpretation

This is an independent empirical exploration. Adoption and family-background comparisons in observational data do not by themselves identify the separate effects of nature and nurture. Access to the licensed source data is required; microdata are not included.
