Empirical exploration

Building rules and housing supply

Using building-permit data to examine story counts, housing rules and candidate policy comparisons.

Area
Developing research
Methods & tools
Python · Open data · Housing
View code on GitHub ↗

Question and implementation

This exploration asks whether restrictions affecting single-stair buildings leave observable patterns in the height and composition of housing construction. Scripts collect and harmonise permit data, construct story-year panels, and examine descriptive and policy-timing comparisons.

What the exploration revealed

Source availability and comparability are central to the design. The existing reports identify differences in permit coverage and a very thin pre-reform sample for one proposed comparison. The exploratory patterns are useful for assessing feasibility, not sufficient to establish a causal housing-supply effect.
