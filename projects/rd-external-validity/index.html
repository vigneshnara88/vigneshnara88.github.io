External validity of regression discontinuity

Frontend and package development for a new approach to extending regression discontinuity beyond the cutoff.

Area
Selected research
Methods & tools
R · Python · Package interfaces

Research direction and credit

The core research ideas and intellectual direction belong to Professor Kirill Borusyak, Dr Luther Yap and Dr Sid George. My role is research support through technical implementation, testing, diagnostics and empirical validation.

Why external validity matters

A regression discontinuity design can identify an effect around a threshold, while a policy decision may concern people elsewhere in the population. The project studies what additional structure and information can help connect these questions.

Frontend and package development

I developed the frontend and worked on packaging the researchers' approach so that it could be used through a clearer interface. The private AIPW_RD repository contains R and Python interfaces, examples, documentation and structured estimation outputs.

This package-development work sits alongside implementation support, comparisons across specifications, diagnostic checks, and figures and tables for research presentations. The methodological contribution belongs to the researchers; my contribution is making the approach easier to use and inspect.

AIPW_RD remains private because it supports unpublished collaborative research. This page describes the software contribution without distributing the research implementation.
